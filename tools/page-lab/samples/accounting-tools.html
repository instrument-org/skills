What would flip it

Over three years Ledgerly comes out cheaper only at 37 invoices a month or fewer. We send 80.

  • Both export to the bank feed Luis already uses
  • Both include payroll for up to five people
  • Tallybook has no mobile receipt capture
The arithmetic

Tallybook: $300 + 36 × $49 = $2,064. Ledgerly: 36 × ($29 + 80 × $0.75) = 36 × $89 = $3,204. Difference: $1,140. Crossing: $300 + $49m = $89m, so m = 7.5.
