Incident review · Tuesday, Oct 20, 14:02 to 14:43

Our own retries took checkout down for 41 minutes

The payment provider rate-limited a few of our calls. Every failed call then retried five times at once, and the limit spread to our whole account.

41 minof failed checkouts

5×retries for each failed call

  1. Pay buttoncustomer taps Pay
  2. checkout.tsbuilds the order
  3. retryPaymentretries 5 times with no wait
  4. Payment provideranswers 429
  5. Error screenasks the customer to try again

Impact

  • No customer was charged twice.
  • We saved every order placed during the outage.
  • Support received 212 tickets.

Timeline

TimeEvent
14:02The provider starts answering 3% of our calls with 429. That is normal during a burst.
14:03Every failed call retries five times at once, and our traffic to the provider triples.
14:09The provider rate-limits our whole account, and every checkout fails.
14:31We turn retries off with the feature flag, and errors start to fall.
14:43The provider lifts the limit, and checkouts go through again.

Fix

When the provider rate-limits a call, it answers 429 with a Retry-After header. We now wait that long and retry once.

- const RETRIES = 5;
- const WAIT_MS = 0;
+ const RETRIES = 1;
+ const waitFor = (res: Response) => Number(res.headers.get("retry-after") ?? 1) * 1000;

Follow-ups

  • Ship the single retry behind the existing flag by Friday.
  • Alert us when 429s pass 1% of calls for two minutes.
  • Correct the status page, which blamed the provider.
