Three changes to try, cheapest first

  1. Send email two on day one, after the first session. One trigger change in the sequence settings.
  2. Fold the setup steps into email one. Only if the first change does not move opens.
  3. Test a shorter subject line on email two. Worth doing alongside either change.

The dashboard shows 34% for email two because it counts every open. The export counts each reader once, which is what this page uses.

How the percentages were worked out

Each email's unique openers divided by 12,400 signups, and by the previous email's openers for the last column. Rounded to whole points.
