Pricing FAQ · Halyard

What the January pricing change means for your bill

For account owners and billing contacts on Starter and Team who received our email this week. Free accounts and Enterprise contracts are not affected. Nothing changes before your first bill dated January 1 or later, and the three questions we are asked most are answered first.

The three questions everyone asks

Why is the price going up?

Because the product is not the one the prices were set for four years ago. Unlimited guests, audit logs, and the API moved from paid add-ons into every plan, and what it costs to run them is the difference. This is the first increase since launch.

What to do: nothing. Read on if you want your exact new amount.

How much more will I pay?

Starter goes from $10 to $12 per seat per month, Team from $20 to $24. A ten-seat Team account on monthly billing pays $40 more a month. Annual prices rise by the same fraction, at your next renewal.

What to do: open Billing, then Plan, to see your account's new amount and the date it applies.

Can I keep my current price?

Yes, for a year. Switch to annual billing before December 31 and today's price holds for the twelve months of that term. After it, renewals are at the new price. Monthly accounts move on the first bill dated January 1 or later.

What to do: Billing, then Plan, then Switch to annual. One click, and it prorates what you have already paid.

Your bill

When will I see the new price?

On the first invoice dated January 1 or later. A monthly account billed on the 15th sees it on January 15. An annual account sees it at its next renewal, whenever in the year that falls.

What to do: check your billing date under Billing, then Invoices.

Will I be charged retroactively?

No. Usage before your first bill at the new price is charged at the old one, including seats you add in December.

What to do: nothing.

Do seat counts or overage rules change?

No. You still pay for active seats only, and a seat removed mid-cycle is credited the same way it is today.

What to do: nothing, unless you want to remove unused seats before January; the Members page sorts by last active.

Will my invoice look different?

Only the unit price line changes. Purchase order numbers, tax handling, and payment methods stay as they are.

What to do: if finance needs the new price on paper, download the updated order form under Billing, then Documents.

Plans and features

What do I get for the increase?

Everything that moved from add-on to included over the last two years: unlimited guest seats, twelve months of audit log, the API and webhooks on every plan, and SSO on Team. If you pay for any of these as add-ons today, those lines drop off your bill in January.

What to do: compare your add-on lines with the plan page; for many Team accounts the net change is smaller than the headline.

Is the Free plan changing?

No. Free stays free, with the same limits.

What to do: nothing.

Should I move from Team to Starter?

Only if you are not using SSO, audit logs older than 30 days, or more than three integrations. Downgrading is instant and reversible; anything above Starter's limits becomes read-only until you upgrade again, and nothing is deleted.

What to do: the Plan page lists which Team features your account used in the last 90 days.

What about Enterprise contracts?

Unchanged until the contract's renewal date, when your account manager proposes new terms. Nothing on this page applies to you before then.

What to do: nothing.

Timing and options

Can I cancel without a penalty?

Yes, at any time, with no cancellation fee. A monthly account stops at the end of the current period; an annual account runs to the end of its term and is not renewed. Your data stays exportable for 90 days after.

What to do: Billing, then Plan, then Cancel. We would rather hear why first: reply to the pricing email.

Is there a discount for nonprofits or schools?

Yes: 30 percent off any plan for registered nonprofits and accredited schools, unchanged by the increase and applied on top of the new prices.

What to do: apply under Billing, then Discounts, with a registration document. Approval takes about two business days.

Will prices go up again next year?

There is no planned increase. Any future change comes with at least 60 days' notice, as this one did.

What to do: nothing.

Can I lock the old price for longer than a year?

Not on Starter or Team. Accounts with 50 or more seats can ask for a two-year term at today's price, which is the Enterprise contract's shape without its minimum.

What to do: reply to the pricing email with your seat count and we will send the term.

Still stuck?

Reply to the pricing email, or write to billing@halyard.example with your account name, and a person on the billing team answers within one business day. If you would rather talk it through, the same email books a 15-minute call. Have your last invoice open; most questions are answered by the two of us reading it together.
