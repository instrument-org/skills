Health

When does my health coverage start?

The first of the month after your start date, or your start date itself if you started on the first. Enrollment closes 30 days after you start. Miss it and the next chance is open enrollment in November, unless you have a qualifying life event such as a marriage, a birth, or losing other coverage.

What do I pay for coverage?

Nothing for yourself, on any of the three medical plans. For a spouse or children, Larkspur pays 80 percent of the premium and the rest comes out of each paycheck before tax. The exact figure for your household appears on the enrollment page once you pick a plan.

Which plan should I pick?

The mid-tier PPO, if you are unsure. Pick the high-deductible plan if you rarely see a doctor and want the health savings account, which Larkspur seeds with $1,000 a year. Pick the low-deductible PPO if you have a known condition or an expected surgery. You can change plans at open enrollment each year.

Are dental and vision separate?

Yes, and each is elected on its own. Dental is free for you and 80 percent covered for dependents, the same as medical. Vision is $6 a month for you and covers one exam and one pair of glasses or a year of contacts.

401(k)

When can I start contributing?

Your first paycheck. Enrollment is automatic at 4 percent of pay unless you change it in the Fidelity portal within your first two weeks. You can raise the rate, lower it, or opt out at any time after that.

What is the match?

Dollar for dollar on the first 4 percent of pay you contribute, deposited every pay period. Contributing less than 4 percent leaves match on the table, which is why 4 is the floor most people set and the default.

When does the match vest?

Immediately. Every dollar Larkspur puts in is yours from the day it lands, including if you leave next month.

Can I roll over an old 401(k)?

Yes, once your account is open, which happens after your first contribution. Start the rollover from the old plan's side; the Fidelity portal shows the address they need. Pre-tax and Roth balances can both come across.

Time off

How much PTO do I get?

20 days a year, accrued through the year from your start date. On top of that: 11 company holidays and the week between Christmas and New Year's Day, which is a company closure and does not count against the 20.

Can I take PTO in my first month?

Yes, up to what you have accrued, and your manager can approve going up to five days negative for a trip you booked before you joined. Raise it in your first one-on-one rather than in the request tool, so it is a conversation and not a surprise.

Does unused PTO carry over?

Up to 10 days carry into the next year; anything above that is lost on December 31. Nobody is paid out for unused days while employed, so plan to use them.

What about sick days?

Separate from PTO: 10 days a year, available from day one, no accrual. Use them for your own illness, a family member's, or a medical appointment. Three or more days in a row needs a note to People Ops, not to your manager.

Parental leave

How long is parental leave?

16 weeks at full pay for every new parent, by birth or adoption, in the child's first year. Birthing parents get an additional 6 weeks of medical leave before the 16 begin, also at full pay.

Do I have to take it all at once?

No. Take it in blocks of at least two weeks, any time in the first twelve months. Most people take the first eight to twelve weeks together and hold the rest for the end of the year.

Do I qualify if I just started?

Yes. There is no waiting period. Leave that begins in your first week is paid the same as leave that begins in your fifth year. State programs may require a work history; People Ops coordinates them on top of the company leave, never instead of it.

Equipment

What equipment do I get?

A laptop from IT before your first day, Mac or Windows, and a $500 home office allowance to spend in your first 90 days. The allowance covers a monitor, a chair, a desk, or whatever your setup is missing. Submit receipts in Expensify under the category Home Office.

Can I get a different laptop?

Within the standard set, yes, by asking in #it-help. Outside it, for a larger screen or more memory, your manager approves the difference and IT orders it. Expect a two-week wait.

What happens to the equipment if I leave?

The laptop goes back to IT; anything bought with the home office allowance is yours to keep. IT sends a prepaid label for the laptop in your last week.
