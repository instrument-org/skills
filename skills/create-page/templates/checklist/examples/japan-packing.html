Packing list

Ten days in Japan in November, packed

Cities most days, one night at an onsen ryokan, and weather that runs from cool mornings to mild afternoons. Everything here fits a carry-on and a daypack if you pack for layers and buy toiletries there.

43 things to pack or know·Before you flycannot be fixed after landing

Clothes

Layers for cool mornings and mild afternoons, and shoes that come off fast.

Documents and money

Everything the trip cannot start without, plus the cash that cities still want.

Tech

The phone does the navigating and the translating, so keep it charged.

Health and toiletries

Bring the medicine you rely on and buy nearly everything else there.

Onsen etiquette

Not things to pack, things to know before the ryokan night.

Leave at home

  • A hair dryer. Every hotel and ryokan has one.
  • Full-size toiletries. The konbini sells everything, in travel sizes.
  • Towels. Provided everywhere you sleep, including the onsen.
  • A voltage converter. Phone and laptop chargers handle it on their own.
  • Dress shoes with laces. See the shoe note above.
  • A second heavy sweater. November is cool, not cold, and rooms are heated.
  • Tipping money. There is no tipping, and it causes confusion.
Based on.
