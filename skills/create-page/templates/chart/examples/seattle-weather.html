Seattle’s rain is not constant, it is seasonal and lumpy

Nov averages 161 mm a year against Jul’s 12, one day in 2015 took 56 mm on its own, and the longest run with no measurable rain at all was 48 days. Three shapes, because no one of them says all three things.

Every wet day, four years at once

One square per day, darker for more rain, blank where none fell. The winters are solid and the summers are gapped; the single darkest square is 2015-03-15.

The spread, not the average

Daily highs by month: the box is the middle half, the line the median, the whiskers the extremes across all four years. July and August are not just warmer, they are tighter.

The year as a wheel

Mean rainfall per month on a radial axis, where December meets January the way the calendar does. A bar chart cuts that join; this does not.
