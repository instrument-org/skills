Chart · 342 birds · Palmer Station, 2007–2009

Longer beaks look shallower, until you split the species

Across all 342 birds, beak length and beak depth are negatively correlated (-0.24). Inside every single species they are positively correlated. Nothing is wrong with either number: the species differ in shape, and a line drawn through all of them measures that instead. Every chart here has its figures in a table under it.

One line, or three

The same 342 points twice. On the left one regression through everything; on the right one per species. The left-hand line is the paradox, and it is the one an ungrouped chart draws by default.

And the sampling behind it

Two of the three species were sampled on one island each, so anything that looks like an island effect here is mostly a species effect. Worth seeing before reading any of the above as geography.
