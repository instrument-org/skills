What earned each score

82Sign-up
Google or email, three fields, median 48 seconds from landing to workspace. Drop-off between the form and the first screen fell from 14 to 6 percent once email verification moved to later.
71First value
Median 6 minutes 20 seconds to a first saved report, down from 11 minutes. One screen, the source connector, still stalls 22 percent of trials for more than two minutes.
79Setup time
The sample-data toggle means 61 percent of trials see a populated dashboard in the first session. The other 39 percent are waiting on a data source and see a blank one.
64Empty states
Five of nine primary screens have an empty state with one action. Alerts, exports, sharing, and the audit log show a blank table and no next step.
58Activation email
Day-one email opens at 44 percent and clicks at 9. The day-three email links to the docs home rather than the user's own workspace and clicks at 3 percent.
77Pricing clarity
The plan page states the seat price and the trial end date on its first screen. Nine tickets this quarter asked what happens at trial end, down from 31.
41Team invite
Invite lives under Settings. 18 percent of trials send one; of those invited, 38 percent open the app within a week. All three peers put invite in the first-run checklist.

What the quarter fixed

  • Deferring email verification removed the largest single drop-off in the funnel.
  • Sample data makes the first session useful for most trials instead of a blank screen.
  • The plan page answers the trial-end question before support has to, and the ticket count shows it.

What still holds it down

  • Invite is buried and invited users mostly never arrive. This is the score that caps team activation, and it is the cheapest to move.
  • The connector step is still the stall point; a third of the time-to-value gain is left on that screen.
  • The day-three email points away from the product at the moment the trial is deciding.
