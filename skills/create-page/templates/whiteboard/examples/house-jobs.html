About this board

An invented house, written to show what a two-axis board is for. Each job sits in the price column its own label names, so the picture cannot drift from the figures; the row is a judgment about how fast it gets worse, and that one is a judgment. Every price here was made up, and a real one comes from a quote. What the board argues is the shape: that the urgent corner is the cheap one, and that two of the dear jobs have an order.
