The opening hue and chroma are those of #c2410c, a burnt orange, read into OKLCH; the ends and the curve are a starting point, not a rule. Steps are placed in OKLCH so that equal moves in lightness look equal, chroma is fullest in the middle and is pulled back wherever sRGB cannot show it, and the hex beside each variable is what the browser will paint. Contrast is WCAG 2's ratio, which is what AA is written against; 4.5 passes for any text, 3 for large text only. The dials are dialkit, which needs the network; the ramp and the variables do not. Nothing you set leaves this page: the dials remember their last position in this browser and nowhere else, and Reset puts them back to the opening setting.
