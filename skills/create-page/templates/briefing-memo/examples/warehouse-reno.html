Briefing memo

Fulfillment: stay in Oakland or move to Reno

To
Dana Whitfield, CEO
From
Marcus Lee, VP Operations
Date
September 8, 2026
Re
Whether to move fulfillment from Oakland to Reno before the lease ends in March 2028

Bottom line up front

Move to Reno in phases: sign a lease by March 2027, ship non-California orders from Reno by June, and keep a small Oakland node for Bay Area same-day until the Oakland lease ends. A phased move captures about 80 percent of the $725,000 a year a full move saves and avoids a hard cutover of 900 orders a day. Decision needed by November 15, 2026 to hold the Reno incentive window.

1.Context

We fulfill from a 48,000 square foot building in Oakland on a lease that ends March 31, 2028. The landlord has offered an early renewal at about $1.60 a square foot, up from $1.45. Volume is 900 orders a day and the 2027 plan calls for 1,400, which the Oakland floor cannot hold past about 1,200 without mezzanine racking or a second site. Reno's industrial market is offering 12 to 18 months of free rent on seven-year terms through early 2027, and both brokers we spoke to expect that window to close once the two distribution centers now under construction there open. Doing nothing means renewing in Oakland at the higher rate in mid-2027, at capacity, with the Reno incentives gone.

2.Key facts

  • 900 orders a day, 5.5 days a week, peaking at 1,600 a day in November and December.
  • Oakland rent: about $835,000 a year for 48,000 square feet at $1.45 per square foot NNN. The early renewal offer is $1.60.
  • Reno rent: about $520,000 a year for 60,000 square feet at $0.72, from three current listings seen this week, before 12 to 18 months of free rent.
  • Labor: about $520,000 a year lower in Reno. 42 warehouse FTE at a loaded $31 an hour in Oakland against about $25 in Reno, at today's rates.
  • Inbound: about $117,000 a year higher from Reno. 180 containers a year land at the Port of Oakland; drayage to Reno adds about $650 each.
  • Net saving of a full move: about $725,000 a year at today's volume, and about $1.0 million at the 2027 plan's 1,400 a day.
  • 62 percent of orders ship to California. Reno reaches the Bay Area in one ground day and Los Angeles in two; Oakland reaches the Bay Area same day, which 14 percent of orders use.
  • 9 of 42 staff would consider relocating. Severance for the rest is about $180,000, and Reno hiring runs 8 to 10 weeks per cohort.
  • One-time cost of moving: $650,000 to $900,000, covering racking, freight, dual running, severance, hiring, and warehouse system reconfiguration.

3.Options

Option A

Stay in Oakland and renew early

Take the $1.60 renewal now and add mezzanine racking for 2027 volume.

Cost
Rent rises about $85,000 a year; about $150,000 once for racking; no move cost.
Risk
Low operationally. The floor is full at 1,200 a day, and the 2027 plan passes that by October.
Forecloses
The Reno incentive window, and growth past 1,200 a day without a second site.

Option B

Full move, hard cutover in Q2 2027

Close Oakland over one weekend once Reno is racked and staffed.

Cost
$650,000 to $900,000 once; saves about $725,000 a year from the first full year. Oakland rent runs to 2028 unless sublet, and sublets recover 60 to 70 percent today.
Risk
Two to three weeks of degraded service at cutover, and 33 experienced staff leaving on the same day.
Forecloses
Bay Area same-day delivery, before we know whether customers pay for it.

Option C · Recommended

Phased move, Oakland kept as a same-day node

Reno takes the 38 percent of orders that leave California by June, then everything but Bay Area same-day by September.

Cost
$800,000 to $1,000,000 once, higher for the longer dual running; saves about $580,000 a year until Oakland closes, then the full $725,000.
Risk
Two nodes need the warehouse system's multi-site module, about six weeks of configuration, and a split inventory rebalanced weekly.
Forecloses
Little. The Oakland node can close in 2028 or stay as a 10,000 square foot same-day room.

4.Recommendation

Option C, the phased move. It captures most of the saving, it moves the risk out of peak, and it keeps the one thing Oakland does that Reno cannot, same-day for the Bay Area, until we know whether customers pay for it. Option B saves $145,000 a year more and buys that with a cutover we have never done at this volume. Option A is the cheapest year and the most expensive decade.

5.Open questions

  1. Will the Oakland landlord accept a partial surrender or a sublease of 38,000 square feet from mid-2027? If not, the phased option carries dual rent a full year longer and its saving narrows to about $400,000. Marcus, this month.
  2. How many of the nine who said they would relocate go once there is an offer on the table? Below five, the Reno launch cohort has no experienced leads. People team, by October 10.
  3. Does the warehouse system's multi-site module hold one inventory pool across two buildings, or two pools with transfers? Two pools doubles safety stock on the top 200 SKUs. Priya, with the vendor.
  4. Does same-day delivery in the Bay Area move retention? We have never measured it. A holdout test in October would answer it before the decision. Growth, by November 1.
  5. What will Reno warehouse wages be once the two distribution centers under construction start hiring in 2027? Our $25 loaded figure is today's rate. Marcus, ask both brokers.
