01 · Context

Harbor cut its Team plan from $49 to $39 a seat per month on Tuesday, September 1, effective immediately for new customers and at renewal for existing ones. Its Business plan did not move. Our Team plan lists at $45 and realizes about $41 after discounts, so Harbor went from 9 percent above us to 13 percent below us in a week. Six business days in, four open deals have asked us to match and two accounts, $71,000 of ARR together, have given notice citing price. Sales wants guidance by Friday because 14 of the exposed deals have proposals due next week, and every day without a position is a day reps improvise one.

What we know about Harbor: their last round was in 2024, press reports put gross margin near 70 percent, and their pricing page shows no end date on the new price. Two customers who buy from both of us say it was presented to them as permanent. A 20 percent list cut at their margin costs them more than waiting costs us.

02 · Options

Option What it is Cost, annual Risk Forecloses
A · Match Cut the Team list price to $36 for new deals and at renewal. $3.6M of ARR at renewal across the base; $1.9M if new deals only, which existing customers notice within a quarter. Starts a price war we have less margin to fight; gross margin falls from 78 to about 72 percent; tells the market the product is worth what Harbor charges. Pricing above Harbor again, and the premium position the roadmap assumes.
B · Hold and add valueRecommended Keep $45. Bundle the $8 reporting add-on into Team for new deals and renewals. Authorize up to 15 percent off for 90 days on renewals tagged price-sensitive, capped at $400,000 in total. About $600,000 of add-on revenue forgone, plus up to $400,000 of retention discounts: about $1.0M at the worst case. We lose 10 to 15 of the 61 exposed deals on price. If Harbor's cut holds a year, a slow leak in accounts under 30 seats. Unbundling reporting later. Little else.
C · New $29 tier Ship a Core tier without automations or integrations at $29 a seat. Team stays at $45. About $250,000 to build and package, then $500,000 to $900,000 a year of Team customers trading down. Three months to ship, past the renewal window. Splits the roadmap, and Harbor can cut again at $29. Nothing durable, but on its own it leaves the next 90 days unanswered.

03 · Recommendation

Option B now, with C scoped for a Q1 decision. The win rate held at 54 percent with a 9 percent price gap in Harbor's favor closed by product. A 13 percent gap the other way is a harder sell, not a lost one, and the reporting bundle is worth $8 a seat to a buyer while costing us far less than a list cut. The retention discount is targeted, capped, and expires; a price cut is none of those. If Harbor is still at $39 in January, the Core tier is the durable answer, and we will have 90 days of win-loss data to size it.
