Every change, oldest first

Date What changed Why What it did to revenue
2021-03-02 Launch pricing. Free for three seats, Starter at $19 per seat, Pro at $49 per seat. Land small teams on Free, sell Pro on automations and reporting. Nearly all first-year revenue came from Pro. Starter was a waiting room, and Free brought support load with almost no conversions.
2022-01-18Turning point Enterprise tier. Custom pricing, annual only, with SSO and the audit log gated behind it. Three inbound deals over a hundred seats asked for SSO in the same quarter. The first six-figure contracts, and the reason there is a sales team. The sales cycle got longer and the forecast got lumpier.
2022-09-13 Starter becomes Team, $19 to $29 for new customers. Existing accounts kept $19 for a year. Support cost per seat was the same on every plan, and Starter was cannibalizing Pro. New-logo volume on the low tier dipped, revenue per new logo rose, and the grandfathering meant no churn spike.
2023-06-06Turning point Free retired, replaced by a 14-day trial of Team. Free accounts were most of the support queue and almost none of the conversions. Signups fell, paid conversions held, so the conversion rate rose. Support tickets from non-payers were gone within a quarter.
2023-09-19 Grandfathered Starter accounts move to $29. The year was up. A churn bump for one quarter, mostly the smallest accounts, then flat. Most stayed.
2024-02-06Turning point Pro becomes Business at $59 per seat with a five-seat minimum, unlimited automations, and an uptime commitment. Pro buyers were mostly teams of five or more, and the minimum ends the two-seat negotiation. Average contract value rose. A slice of one-to-four-seat Pro accounts dropped to Team, which showed as downgrade revenue for a quarter.
2024-10-01 Annual discount trimmed from 20 percent to two months free. Margin. The discount was larger than the collections benefit justified. Nothing visible. The annual mix did not move and nobody wrote in.
2025-04-15 AI replies add-on, metered per resolved conversation, on Team and Business. Model costs scale with usage, not seats, so seat pricing would have lost money on heavy users. The first non-seat revenue, small and growing every month. Customers found bills harder to predict, so finance added a monthly cap the customer sets.
2026-01-13 Team $29 to $35 for new customers. Renewals move at their next anniversary. Business unchanged. First list increase in three years, in line with costs. Too early to say. Watch the renewal cohort from April onward, and expect every prospect to ask.

Where this leaves you, September 2026

Team

$35 per seat, monthly

Accounts from before January pay $29 until their next anniversary. Check the date before you quote a renewal.

Business

$59 per seat, five-seat minimum

Pro with unlimited automations and an uptime commitment. The minimum is the only thing a former Pro customer lost.

Enterprise

Custom annual only

SSO and the audit log live here and nowhere else. That gate is the whole reason the tier exists.

AI replies

Metered per resolved conversation

On Team and Business, with a monthly cap the customer sets. When a bill surprises someone, this is why.

What a prospect will ask

  • Why did Team go up? First increase in three years, on a product that gained the 2024 automations in between.
  • Can I have Pro back? Business is Pro with more in it. A team under five seats belongs on Team, and most of them found that out in 2024.
  • Why does my bill vary? AI replies. Point them to the cap.

Cohorts still on old terms

  • Team accounts opened before January 13, 2026, at $29 until their next anniversary; all of them will have moved by January 2027.
  • Annual contracts signed before October 2024 still carry the 20 percent discount until they renew.
  • Nobody is on Free, Starter, or Pro. If a customer says they are, they mean Team or Business and remember the old name.
