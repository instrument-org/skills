Drive · 12 stops

San Francisco to Los Angeles on Highway 1, in two days

The coast road with a night in Cambria at the halfway point, so Big Sur is driven in daylight. Times are driving only.

  2. 1

    Golden Gate Bridge Welcome Center

    San Francisco · the start

    Coffee, the bridge from the south side, and Highway 1 begins a few minutes down Lincoln Boulevard. Leave by nine to make Big Sur in daylight.

    goldengate.orgGoogle Maps

    49 mi · 1 h 20 min to the next stop

  3. 2

    Pigeon Point Lighthouse

    Pescadero · a stop of twenty minutes

    The tallest lighthouse on the West Coast, on a bluff with the road running past it. Stretch, look for whales in season, and keep going.

    parks.ca.gov/?page_id=533Google Maps

    71 mi · 1 h 35 min to the next stop

  4. 3

    Monterey Bay Aquarium

    Cannery Row, Monterey · two hours, book ahead

    The kelp forest tank and the sea otters. Lunch on Cannery Row after, because the next stretch has almost nowhere to eat.

    montereybayaquarium.orgGoogle Maps

    20 mi · 30 min to the next stop

  5. 4

    Bixby Creek Bridge

    Big Sur · a pull-out

    The concrete arch of every Big Sur postcard. Pull off at the north end; the view is from the turnout, not the bridge.

    Google Maps

    16 mi · 25 min to the next stop

  6. 5

    Nepenthe

    Big Sur · a late lunch or an early dinner

    The terrace over the ocean is the reason; the Ambrosia burger is the order. It fills by noon in summer.

    nepenthe.comGoogle Maps

    8 mi · 10 min to the next stop

  7. 6

    McWay Falls

    Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park · a half-hour walk

    An eighty-foot fall onto a beach nobody can reach, seen from a short trail off the highway. Park in the lot, or on the shoulder if it is full.

    parks.ca.gov/?page_id=578Google Maps

    48 mi · 1 h 05 min to the next stop

  8. 7

    Elephant Seal Vista Point

    Piedras Blancas · a boardwalk

    Hundreds of elephant seals on the beach below a free boardwalk, all year, loudest in winter. The docents in blue jackets will tell you what you are looking at.

    elephantseal.orgGoogle Maps

    13 mi · 20 min to the next stop

  9. 8

    Cambria

    Overnight · the halfway point

    Sleep here, at the end of the Big Sur stretch. Moonstone Beach for the evening; Hearst Castle is ten minutes back up the road if you want it in the morning.

    visitcambriaca.comGoogle Maps

    22 mi · 35 min to the next stop

  10. Day 2 · Cambria to Santa MonicaDirections
  11. 9

    Morro Rock

    Morro Bay · a stop of half an hour

    A 576-foot volcanic plug at the harbor mouth, with the otters in the water beside it. Drive out to the base; you cannot climb it.

    morrobay.orgGoogle Maps

    109 mi · 2 h 25 min to the next stop

  12. 10

    Stearns Wharf

    Santa Barbara · lunch

    The road has been the 101 since Pismo, and rejoins the coast here. Walk the wharf, eat, and take the last stretch through Malibu in the afternoon light.

    stearnswharf.orgGoogle Maps

    60 mi · 1 h 15 min to the next stop

  13. 11

    El Matador State Beach

    Malibu · the last beach

    Sea stacks and caves down a steep path from a small lot. The last hour of Highway 1 runs along the water from here to the pier.

    parks.ca.gov/?page_id=633Google Maps

    24 mi · 40 min to the next stop

  14. 12

    Santa Monica Pier

    Los Angeles · the end

    Where Route 66 ends and this drive does too. Ferris wheel, sunset, and the traffic of a city that has been waiting for you.

    santamonicapier.orgGoogle Maps
