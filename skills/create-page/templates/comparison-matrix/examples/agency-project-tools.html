How to read this

  • Read the Billing hourly band first. It is the only band where the six tools genuinely differ, and it is the reason the agency asked.
  • Count tones, not features. No column is all green: Teamwork and Productive win the billing rows and lose setup, ClickUp wins price and loses invoicing, Basecamp wins simplicity and loses hours.
  • A warning tone means weak for this brief, not a bad product. Asana's missing invoicing is a flaw for an agency that bills hourly and irrelevant for one on retainer.
  • A footnote number points at the catch behind a cell: a seat pack, a tier boundary, an add-on with its own price.
Sources.
