How to read this

  • Open the band that matches the trip. A city week with a small bag is Size and weight and Lenses; a hiking trip is sealing and stabilization; kids or street is Autofocus.
  • Count stars, then check their tone. The a6700 takes the most rows and the most money; the X-T30 II takes size and price and loses stabilization, sealing, and battery; the OM-5 is the only one you would carry in the rain.
  • A warning tone is a weakness for travel, not a verdict on the camera. No stabilization matters at dusk in a cathedral and not at all at noon on a beach.
  • The two price rows are the same cap seen twice: body only, and with the kit zoom that makes it a camera.
