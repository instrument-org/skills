What it assumes. A repayment loan at a rate that never changes, one overpayment a month on top of the normal payment, and every extra pound going straight at the balance.

Where it is wrong. A fixed rate that ends and resets, an early-repayment charge, an annual overpayment cap, or a lender that holds overpayments as a credit rather than reducing the balance. Any of the four changes the answer, and the last one changes it a lot.

Where the defaults came from. Nowhere. $280,000 at 5.9% over 24 years is a shape that makes the curves legible, not an offer, a market average, or anybody's actual loan.
